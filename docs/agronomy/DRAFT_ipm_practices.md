# DRAFT: non-chemical IPM / orchard practices for Nagpur mandarin (santra) and mosambi

> **Status: DRAFT. Not expert-reviewed. Do not seed or show to growers until an agronomist approves.**
> Fetched and read on 2026-10-26, in this session only. Every item below comes from a page listed in
> "Sources fetched". Wording is a summary; short quotes are marked with quotation marks.
> **Owner rule: no chemical advice.** Pesticides, fungicides, PGRs, copper/Bordeaux, sulphur, neem and
> other botanical or registered bio-pesticide products, fertiliser doses and spray schedules were
> left out on purpose. Where a source gives such a product, the item says only "a registered product
> exists; see label information". Most items were written for Nagpur mandarin. Unless an item says
> mosambi or sweet orange, its use on mosambi is a general-citrus reading that a reviewer must confirm.

## 1. Site, soil and planting material (prevention of decline)

1. **Choose deep, loamy, well-drained soil; avoid saline, alkaline or lime-nodule soils.** — Source: C12, "Soil". Evidence: Official.
   मराठी: खोल, निचऱ्याची, पोयट्याची जमीन निवडा; खारट, चोपण किंवा चुनखडीच्या खड्यांची जमीन टाळा.
2. **Before planting, test soil for lime: plant santra only where CaCO3 is below 6 % and carbonate clay is below 2 %.** — Source: R12 p.2 #12 (matches P2, 2012). Evidence: University.
   मराठी: लागवडीपूर्वी माती तपासा; चुनखडी ६ टक्क्यांपेक्षा कमी असलेल्या जमिनीतच संत्रा लावा.
3. **Plant only genuine, disease-free grafts from certified budwood**, to guard against greening, exocortis and decline. — Source: C02 "Greening", "Citrus Exocortis"; C03 "Citrus Dieback/Decline"; R25 p.14 #57 (genuine planting material is a major gap). Evidence: Official + University.
   मराठी: खात्रीच्या रोपवाटिकेतूनच, रोगमुक्त डोळ्यापासून बनवलेली कलमे घ्या.
4. **Keep the bud union at least 15 cm above ground at planting; firm the soil, irrigate at once, stake in strong wind.** — Source: C10 "Method of Planting". Evidence: Official.
   मराठी: कलम लावताना डोळ्याचा जोड जमिनीपासून किमान १५ सें.मी. वर ठेवा; लगेच पाणी द्या, वाऱ्यात काठीचा आधार द्या.
5. **Use Phytophthora-tolerant rootstocks**: Rangpur lime and rough lemon (Akola), and Alemow (*Citrus microphylla*) as a root-rot-tolerant rootstock. — Source: P1 Plant Pathology recs; R25 p.10 #29; C11 rootstock table (Rangpur lime tolerant to foot rot). Evidence: University + Official.
   मराठी: डिंक्या/मुळकुज टाळण्यासाठी रंगपूर लाइम, जंबेरी (अकोला) किंवा अलेमो खुंटावरची कलमे निवडा.
6. **High-density planting at 6 m x 3 m on raised beds** (Indo-Israel method) for Nagpur mandarin. — Source: R16 p.4 #21; R25 p.15 #62 (economics). Evidence: University.
   मराठी: उंच गादीवाफ्यावर ६ x ३ मीटर अंतरावर घन लागवड करता येते.
7. **Traditional spacing about 6 m x 6 m** for both mosambi and Nagpur mandarin. — Source: C10 "Spacing"; N1 §5.5.3. Evidence: Official.
   मराठी: पारंपरिक लागवडीत मोसंबी व संत्र्यासाठी ६ x ६ मीटर अंतर.

## 2. Nursery hygiene

8. **Soil solarisation of nursery beds for 45 days (Sept–Nov) under clear transparent polythene**, against diseases and annual weeds. (The seed treatment in the same item is excluded.) — Source: P1 Plant Pathology recs. Evidence: University.
   मराठी: रोपवाटिकेचे वाफे पारदर्शक प्लास्टिकने ४५ दिवस झाकून उन्हात तापवा.
9. **Cover the rootstock stem with a strip of used fertiliser bag or black polyethylene tape** instead of rubbing off side shoots by hand. — Source: R23 p.6 #23. Evidence: University.
   मराठी: खुंटावरील फुटवे काढत बसण्यापेक्षा खुंट रिकाम्या खताच्या पिशवीच्या पट्टीने किंवा काळ्या टेपने गुंडाळा.
10. **Cover fresh bud grafts with 70 % agro-net** (from budding in February) to raise growth and cut graft mortality. — Source: P1 Horticulture recs. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: डोळे भरल्यानंतर कलमांवर ७० टक्के शेडनेट झाका.

## 3. Canopy: training, pruning, sanitation

11. **Train to a single stem with 4–6 well-spaced main branches; keep the trunk clear of branches up to about 45–50 cm.** — Source: N1 §5.8 (50 cm); C05 "Training and Pruning" (45 cm). Evidence: Official. (Sources differ slightly.)
    मराठी: एक खोड ठेवून ४–६ चांगल्या पसरलेल्या फांद्या ठेवा; खोडावर ४५–५० सें.मी. पर्यंत फांदी ठेवू नका.
12. **On bearing trees, remove dead, diseased, criss-cross and weak branches, water sprouts and rootstock suckers, preferably after harvest.** — Source: N1 §5.8. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळतोडणीनंतर वाळलेल्या, रोगट, एकमेकांत घुसलेल्या फांद्या, पाणफुटवे आणि खुंटावरचे फुटवे काढा.
13. **Support heavily loaded branches with a bamboo frame and rope** so they do not break. — Source: C05 "Training and Pruning". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळांनी लदबदलेल्या फांद्या बांबूच्या चौकटीला दोरीने बांधा.
14. **High-density orchards: prune at 10 ft height in the third week of December** for the ambia crop. — Source: R16 p.4 #22. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: घन लागवडीत आंबिया बहारासाठी डिसेंबरच्या तिसऱ्या आठवड्यात १० फूट उंचीवर छाटणी करा.
15. **Rejuvenate declining orchards with medium pruning in June** (terminal shoots cut back up to 45 cm). The same item adds fertiliser and fungicide after pruning; those are excluded. — Source: P1 Horticulture recs. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: खंगलेल्या बागेत जूनमध्ये फांद्यांची टोके ४५ सें.मी. पर्यंत छाटून बागेला नवसंजीवनी द्या.
16. **Remove dead twigs (twig blight), diseased twigs (anthracnose), affected branches (sooty mould) and diseased portions (pink disease) and destroy them.** — Source: C02 sections Twig blight, Anthracnose, Sooty mould, Pink disease. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: वाळलेल्या, रोगट फांद्या कापून बागेबाहेर नेऊन नष्ट करा.
17. **Collect and destroy diseased leaves, twigs and fruits (scab).** — Source: C02 "Scab". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: खरूज रोगाची पाने, फांद्या, फळे गोळा करून नष्ट करा.
18. **Disinfect grafting and pruning tools after cutting diseased trees** (exocortis). No product is named in the source. — Source: C02 "Citrus Exocortis". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: रोगट झाड छाटल्यानंतर कात्री, चाकू निर्जंतुक करूनच पुढच्या झाडाला लावा.

## 4. Phytophthora: gummosis, foot / collar rot, root rot

19. **Choose a site with good drainage; dig a drainage channel between rows** (source size about 60 cm wide x 30 cm deep). — Source: C02 "Foot Rot or Gummosis"; P1 "Transfer of Technology"; R25 p.14 #57 (drainage is the biggest gap). Evidence: Official + University.
    मराठी: बागेत पाणी साचू देऊ नका; दोन ओळींमध्ये निचऱ्यासाठी चर खणा.
20. **Use ring (double-ring) irrigation so water does not touch the trunk.** — Source: C02 "Foot Rot or Gummosis". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: खोडाला पाणी लागू नये म्हणून आळ्याऐवजी खोडापासून दूर गोल (रिंग) पद्धतीने पाणी द्या.
21. **For an infected tree: remove all its fruits, prune dried branches with about 5 cm of healthy wood, and cut shoots back 30–45 cm.** — Source: P1 "Management of Phytophthora Root Rot Infected Plants". Evidence: University.
    मराठी: रोगग्रस्त झाडावरची सर्व फळे काढा; वाळलेल्या फांद्या ५ सें.मी. निरोगी भागासह कापा.
22. **Expose the active root zone, cut out rotten roots and leave the zone open to sun for 3–4 days.** The drench, paste and spray steps in the same package are excluded. — Source: P1 "Management of Phytophthora Root Rot Infected Plants". Evidence: University.
    मराठी: झाडाभोवतीची माती उकरून कुजलेली मुळे कापा आणि ३–४ दिवस मुळांना ऊन लागू द्या.
23. **At gummosis onset PDKV recommends soil-applied bio-control agents mixed with FYM at the tree periphery.** A registered product exists; see label information. — Source: R21 p.6 #23; P1 Plant Pathology recs (greenhouse comparison of bio-agents). Evidence: University.
    मराठी: डिंक्या सुरू झाल्यावर जैविक बुरशीनाशकाचा पर्याय आहे; नोंदणीकृत उत्पादनाच्या लेबलप्रमाणेच वापरा.
24. **Trunk protection twice a year (before and after the monsoon) is recommended.** A registered product exists; see label information. — Source: R20 p.5 #19; R21 p.6 #23; P1. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: पावसाळ्यापूर्वी व नंतर खोडाला संरक्षक लेप लावण्याची शिफारस आहे; उत्पादन लेबलप्रमाणे वापरा.

## 5. Psylla and greening

25. **Conserve natural enemies: coccinellid (ladybird) beetles feed on psylla nymphs.** — Source: C08 "Citrus Psylla". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: लेडीबर्ड (ढालकीडा) सिट्रस सायलाची पिल्ले खातो; त्यांना मारू नका.
26. **Hang yellow sticky traps from the first week of June, about 2 m above ground, and change them monthly** (source: 30 per acre). Taken from a mixed module; its chemical parts are excluded. — Source: R25 p.10 #32. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: जूनच्या पहिल्या आठवड्यापासून जमिनीपासून २ मीटर उंचीवर पिवळे चिकट सापळे लावा, दर महिन्याला बदला.
27. **Watch new flush closely**: psylla, black fly and leaf miner all attack new flush; PDKV ties its psylla actions to the new flush. — Source: R13 p.3 #18; R25 p.10 #32; C08 "Citrus Leaf Miner". Evidence: University + Official.
    मराठी: नवती फुटली की पानांच्या टोकांवर सायला, काळी माशी, पाने पोखरणारी अळी आहे का ते रोज पाहा.
28. **Greening spreads by grafting: use budwood only from greening-free parent trees.** — Source: C02 "Greening". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: ग्रीनिंग कलमांतून पसरतो; रोगमुक्त मातृवृक्षाचेच डोळे वापरा.

## 6. Citrus black fly

29. **Scout the shady side of the canopy first**; the attack is heavier there, and sooty mould on leaves is a sign. — Source: C08 "Citrus White Fly / Citrus Black Fly". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: झाडाची सावलीकडची बाजू आधी तपासा; पानांवर काळी बुरशी दिसली तर काळी माशी असू शकते.
30. **Time scouting to egg hatch: in Katol, over half the eggs hatched in early April, late July and early December** (ambia, mrig, hasta flushes). Survival was highest in the hasta-flush brood. — Source: P1 Entomology recs. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: काटोल भागात एप्रिलचा पहिला पंधरवडा, जुलैचा दुसरा व डिसेंबरचा पहिला पंधरवडा हा अंडी उबण्याचा काळ; तेव्हा बारकाईने पाहा.
31. **Biological control: two releases of the green lacewing *Mallada boninensis* (eggs on shoots) during hasta bahar** (release rate in source). — Source: P1 Entomology recs. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: हस्त बहारात क्रायसोपा (मलाडा) या मित्रकीटकाची अंडी फांद्यांवर दोनदा सोडा.
32. **Recognise and protect the beneficial fungus *Hypocrella raciborskii* (Aschersonia) on black fly nymphs.** A registered product exists for spraying; see label information. — Source: R25 p.10 #31; R25 p.11 #38 (research finding). Evidence: University.
    मराठी: काळ्या माशीच्या पिल्लांवर वाढणारी मित्रबुरशी (ॲशरसोनिया) ओळखा आणि जपा.

## 7. Leaf miner, lemon butterfly, bark borer, scales, nematode

33. **Leaf miner: prune all affected parts in winter.** — Source: C08 "Citrus Leaf Miner" (it also notes leaf-miner damage favours canker). Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: पाने पोखरणाऱ्या अळीने खराब झालेले भाग हिवाळ्यात छाटून टाका.
34. **Lemon butterfly: hand-pick the green caterpillars**, especially in nurseries and on young flush. — Source: C08 "Citrus or Lemon Butterfly". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: रोपवाटिकेत व नवतीवर लिंबू फुलपाखराच्या हिरव्या अळ्या हाताने वेचून नष्ट करा.
35. **Bark borer: look for webbing of chewed wood and frass on the trunk and remove it** (Sept–Oct and Jan–Feb). The hole treatment in the source uses a banned chemical and is excluded. — Source: C08 "Bark Borer". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: खोडावर भुसा व विष्ठेचे जाळे दिसले तर साल पोखरणारी अळी आहे; जाळे काढून छिद्र तपासा.
36. **Soft scales are worse with poor light from overcrowding and waterlogging**; spacing, pruning and drainage address those conditions (reading drawn from the source's statement). — Source: C08 "Scale Insects". Evidence: Official (inferred practice).
    मराठी: दाट, अंधारी आणि पाणथळ बागेत खवले कीड वाढते; हवा, ऊन खेळते ठेवा.
37. **Nematode: certified nematode-free planting material, good sanitation, 1–3 years of annual crops before replanting citrus, tolerant rootstocks (trifoliate orange, citranges), and a marigold intercrop.** — Source: C08 "Citrus Nematode"; C11 rootstock table. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: सूत्रकृमी टाळण्यासाठी सूत्रकृमीमुक्त कलमे, जुनी बाग काढल्यावर १–३ वर्षे इतर पिके, आणि झेंडूचे आंतरपीक घ्या.

## 8. Fruit fly and fruit-sucking moth

38. **Collect and destroy fallen and infested fruits regularly.** — Source: C08 "Fruit Flies", "Fruit Sucking Moths". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: गळलेली व किडलेली फळे रोज गोळा करून खोल पुरा किंवा नष्ट करा.
39. **Fruit fly: lure (parapheromone) traps from early October, with the lure changed every 15–20 days.** The lure is a product; see label information (trap density in source). — Source: R25 p.10 #32. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: ऑक्टोबरपासून फळमाशीसाठी ल्युअर सापळे लावा; ल्युअर १५–२० दिवसांनी बदला.
40. **Fruit-sucking moth: remove its alternate host plants (weeds) from around the orchard**; the moths hide in fallen leaves and weeds by day. — Source: C08 "Fruit Sucking Moths". Evidence: Official. (The poison bait in C08 and R25 is excluded.)
    मराठी: बागेभोवतीचे पतंगाचे पर्यायी यजमान तण काढा; पडलेला पालापाचोळा साफ ठेवा.

## 9. Canker, decline, dieback, tristeza, other root diseases

41. **Canker: before the monsoon, prune and burn all canker-infected twigs.** The source also protects the cuts with a product; a registered product exists, see label information. — Source: C02 "Citrus Canker". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: पावसाळ्यापूर्वी कँकरग्रस्त फांद्या छाटून जाळा.
42. **Decline / dieback: right site and soil, certified budwood, proper rootstock, and careful irrigation and manuring.** — Source: C03 "Citrus Dieback/Decline". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: खंगणे टाळायला योग्य जमीन, खात्रीची कलमे, योग्य खुंट आणि मोजके पाणी-खत हेच मूळ उपाय.
43. **Twig-blight risk forecast from weather** (max/min temperature, morning/evening humidity, wind, rain, sunshine) predicts incidence in advance. Candidate for an in-app weather alert. — Source: R14 p.6 #17 (equation in source). Evidence: University.
    मराठी: हवामानावरून शेंडेमर (फांदी करपा) येण्याचा अंदाज आधी मिळतो; सूचना आल्यावर बाग तपासा.
44. **Tristeza: good cultural practice, better soil fertility and good drainage reduce decline.** Mosambi note: a PDKV survey found more CTV in sweet orange than in Nagpur mandarin. — Source: C02 "Tristeza"; P1 Plant Pathology recs (survey 1997–2006). Evidence: Official + University.
    मराठी: ट्रिस्टेझा टाळायला चांगली मशागत, सुपीक जमीन व निचरा ठेवा; मोसंबीत हा विषाणू जास्त आढळतो.
45. **Ganoderma root rot: remove dead and old stumps, collect and destroy the bracket fungi near the collar, and isolate infected trees with a trench.** — Source: C02 "Ganoderma Root-Rot". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: बागेतील जुने बुंधे काढा; खोडाजवळ उगवणाऱ्या कानासारख्या बुरशी नष्ट करा; रोगट झाडाभोवती चर खणा.
46. **Dry root rot: if only one or two roots are affected, cut them off and destroy them.** — Source: C02 "Dry Root Rot". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: एक-दोनच मुळे कुजली असतील तर ती कापून नष्ट करा.

## 10. Fruit drop, splitting and other disorders

47. **Know the drop waves: the marble stage (about a month after full bloom), the May–June heat, and pre-harvest (Aug–Jan).** Causes include lack of fertilisation (fruit set), mechanical shock, pests, disease, heat, rain and faulty practices. — Source: C03 "Fruit Drop". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळगळ तीन वेळा होते: गोटी अवस्था, मे-जूनची उष्णता आणि तोडणीपूर्व; कारण ओळखूनच उपाय करा.
48. **Do not let the tree run short of water just before flowering and after fruit set**; a shortage then sheds flowers and young fruit. — Source: C06. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फुलोऱ्याआधी आणि फळधारणेनंतर पाण्याचा ताण पडू देऊ नका, नाहीतर फूल-फळगळ होते.
49. **Fruit-drop training should stress drainage, soil selection, nutrition, genuine planting material and pest and disease management**, the largest adoption gaps found. — Source: R25 p.14 #57. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: फळगळ कमी करण्यासाठी निचरा, योग्य जमीन, संतुलित खत, खात्रीची कलमे यांवर आधी लक्ष द्या.
50. **Fruit splitting: give frequent light irrigations in hot, dry spells and pick fruit soon after maturity.** — Source: C03 "Fruit Splitting". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळ तडकू नये म्हणून कडक उन्हात थोडे-थोडे पण वारंवार पाणी द्या आणि फळे पक्व होताच तोडा.

## 11. Bahar treatment (water stress) and irrigation

51. **Bahar treatment: withhold water until the leaves wilt.** For deeper soils the source adds root exposure: about 7–10 cm of soil removed within a 40–60 cm radius. Then re-cover the roots with soil mixed with FYM and irrigate at once. — Source: N1 §5.13. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: बहारासाठी पाणी तोडा, पाने कोमेजेपर्यंत ताण द्या; नंतर मुळांवर शेणखत मिसळलेली माती टाकून लगेच पाणी द्या.
52. **Do not expose roots in light, sandy or shallow soils; withholding water for 2–3 weeks is enough there.** — Source: N1 §5.13. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: हलक्या, उथळ जमिनीत मुळे उघडी करू नका; २–३ आठवडे पाणी तोडणे पुरेसे.
53. **Stress length: ambia about 40–60 days (mid-Nov to end-Dec); mrig about 40–50 days in May–June (to about 25 % leaf drop); PDKV: 50 days for mrig on medium soil.** — Source: C05 "Regulation of Flowering"; P1 Horticulture recs. Evidence: Official + University. (N1 says "about a month"; the sources differ.)
    मराठी: आंबियासाठी साधारण ४०–६० दिवस, मृगासाठी ४०–५० दिवस ताण; मध्यम जमिनीत मृगासाठी ५० दिवस पुरे.
54. **Prefer drip irrigation**: it saves water, wets only the root zone and reduces weeds; PDKV found about 30 % water saving with bigger, juicier fruit. — Source: N1 §5.7.1; C06; P1 Horticulture recs. Evidence: Official + University.
    मराठी: ठिबक सिंचन वापरा: पाणी वाचते, तण कमी, फळे मोठी व रसाळ.
55. **Schedule drip at 80 % of evapotranspiration replenishment through all growth stages** (central and western Vidarbha). — Source: R15 p.4 #14. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: ठिबकने बाष्पीभवनाच्या ८० टक्के इतके पाणी सर्व अवस्थांत द्या.
56. **Surface irrigation on medium soil: irrigate at 40 % soil-moisture depletion** (about 60 mm cumulative pan evaporation). — Source: P1 Horticulture recs. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: मध्यम जमिनीत ओलावा ४० टक्के कमी झाला की पाणी द्या.
57. **Sprinkler irrigation can replace basin irrigation and save water** (per-tree volumes for winter and summer are in source). — Source: P1 Horticulture recs; C06. Evidence: University + Official.
    मराठी: आळे पद्धतीऐवजी तुषार सिंचनाने पाणी वाचवता येते.
58. **Water at fruit maturity too, to avoid fruit shrinkage and dry pulp**; mild late stress raises TSS but reduces size. — Source: C06. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळ पक्व होताना पाणी तोडू नका, नाहीतर फळ आकसते व रस सुकतो.

## 12. Soil cover, organic matter, weeds

59. **Mulch the basin with dry leaves, paddy husk or wheat/paddy straw** (about 5–8 cm): it keeps down weeds, saves irrigation and conserves moisture. — Source: N1 §5.10 (about 8 cm); P1 Horticulture recs (5 cm straw kept about 17.6 % more moisture). Evidence: Official + University.
    मराठी: आळ्यात वाळलेली पाने, भाताचे तूस किंवा गव्हाचा/भाताचा पेंढा ५–८ सें.मी. पसरा.
60. **Grow a green manure crop (e.g. sunhemp) in kharif and plough it in before flowering.** — Source: P1 Horticulture recs; C05 "Intercropping". Evidence: University + Official.
    मराठी: खरिपात ताग पेरून फुलोऱ्याआधी जमिनीत गाडा.
61. **Supply part of the nitrogen through organic manure (FYM, cakes)** (quantities in source, not given here). — Source: N1 §5.6. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: खताचा काही भाग शेणखत/पेंडीतून द्या (मात्रा तज्ज्ञांकडून घ्या).
62. **Weeds: shallow cultivation only (no deeper than 10–15 cm), plus mulch.** Herbicide items in sources are excluded. — Source: C05 "Weed Control". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: तणासाठी उथळ (१०–१५ सें.मी.) खुरपणी करा; खोल नांगरट मुळांना हानी करते.
63. **Intercropping: sources conflict.** N1 allows pea, cowpea and gram, and PDKV found cowpea profitable. C05 says to avoid intercrops in Nagpur mandarin (more pests, diseases and micronutrient deficiency) and allows short crops only in the first 3–4 years elsewhere. — Source: N1 §5.11; P1; C05. Evidence: Official + University (conflict, reviewer to decide).
    मराठी: आंतरपिकाबाबत मतभेद आहेत; संत्र्यात आंतरपीक घेण्यापूर्वी तज्ज्ञांचा सल्ला घ्या.

## 13. Harvest and post-harvest hygiene

64. **Harvest mandarin when about 75 % of the surface has turned yellowish-orange; check the TSS:acid ratio too.** — Source: C04. Evidence: Official. (C04 also gives sweet orange signs: light-yellow, shiny skin, softer rind.)
    मराठी: संत्र्याच्या सालीचा ७५ टक्के भाग पिवळसर-केशरी झाल्यावर तोडणी करा.
65. **Cut fruit with clippers or secateurs, leaving a little stalk; never pull by hand; do not harvest in wet weather.** — Source: C04; N1 §5.15. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळे कात्रीने देठासह तोडा, हाताने ओढू नका; पावसात किंवा ओल्या हवामानात तोडणी करू नका.
66. **Handle gently and keep picked fruit in shade**; cuts and bruises let storage rots in, and rough handling causes oleocellosis. — Source: C02 "Storage Rots"; C03 "Oleocellosis"; C04. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: फळे हळुवार हाताळा, सावलीत ठेवा; खरचटलेले फळ लवकर सडते.
67. **Cure fruit overnight before moving it to the pack house** to reduce oleocellosis (temperature in source). — Source: C03 "Oleocellosis". Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: तोडलेली फळे एक रात्र थंड जागी ठेवून मगच पॅकहाऊसला न्या.
68. **Sort out damaged, decayed and diseased fruit before packing.** — Source: C09 "Grading"; N1 §6.1. Evidence: Official.
    मराठी: पॅकिंगपूर्वी खराब, सडलेली, रोगट फळे वेगळी काढा.
69. **Fungicide-free UV-C treatment of fruit before waxing** (PDKV UV-assisted device) cuts Penicillium and Geotrichum rots in Nagpur mandarin and, at a slower conveyor speed, in **sweet orange (mosambi)**. — Source: R17 p.11 #28; R22 p.2 #6; R23 p.8 #45. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: पॅकहाऊसमध्ये बुरशीनाशकाऐवजी यूव्ही-सी प्रकाश यंत्राने फळांची सड कमी करता येते, मोसंबीलाही चालते.
70. **UV-C tubes behind the air-handling unit of mandarin cold rooms** reduce airborne microbes and rotting. — Source: R24 p.10 #55. Evidence: University.
    मराठी: शीतगृहात हवेच्या यंत्रामागे यूव्ही-सी ट्यूब लावल्यास साठवलेली संत्री कमी सडतात.

## Gaps (no non-chemical practice found in the fetched sources)

- **Mites (citrus mite, russeting):** C08, R13 and P1 give chemical controls only.
- **Aphids (tristeza vector), thrips, mealybugs:** chemical only (C08); N1 only lists them.
- **Psylla, direct control:** only natural-enemy conservation and sticky traps; no threshold or scouting count given.
- **Leaf miner beyond winter pruning:** remaining items are chemical or botanical sprays (excluded).
- **Fruit drop from heat (May–June wave):** causes described; the only remedies are PGR/fungicide sprays (N1 §5.12, C03, R22, R24, R25), all excluded.
- **Waibar disorder, greasy spot, powdery mildew, Alternaria, leaf fall / fruit rot (P. palmivora), greening (disease):** chemical or fertiliser schedules only (R22, R24, R25, C02).
- **Canker, biological option:** P1 notes lab activity of a marigold (Tagetes) leaf extract on canker bacteria; a lab finding, not a field practice.
- **Mosambi-specific IPM:** apart from UV-C post-harvest (R23 #45), sweet-orange maturity signs (C04), spacing (C10) and the CTV note (P1), nothing in the fetched sources was specific to mosambi.
- **Economic thresholds / scouting protocols** for any citrus pest: none found.
- **Nutrition as practice:** organic/bio-input packages exist (R14 #20, R16 #24), but all are tied to fertiliser doses and products, so they were excluded.
- **ICAR-CCRI and NIPHM content** (monthly advisories, citrus IPM package): not fetched (hosts not reachable); expected to fill several gaps above.
